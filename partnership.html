Partnership & Collaboration

30+ partners across Nepal and internationally

Malpi International College partners with leading organisations in banking, insurance, technology, advisory, energy, hospitality and industry.

These are working relationships rather than logos on a wall. Partners host our interns, set live consulting briefs, teach at the college, sit on the Malpi Synergy Council and recruit directly from our graduating cohorts, which is what stands behind our 100% placement support.
