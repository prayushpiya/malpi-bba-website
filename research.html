Student research

Research runs through every programme

Research is not reserved for postgraduates. Each Malpi degree carries a dedicated research methods course and culminates in supervised project work.

1

BBA: Business Research Methods (RCH 110)

Semester IV introduces research design, data collection and analysis, supported by an Academic Writing practicum (PRC 114).

2

BCSIT: Research Methods (RCH 322)

Semester VI covers research methods ahead of the Major Project (PRJ 481) in the final year.

3

MBA: Methods for Business Research and Consultancy (RES 611)

Postgraduate students choose the Research Pathway (Graduate Thesis, RES 612, 6 credits) or the Industry Pathway’s Consulting Project.

Applied, not abstract

Research with an industry problem attached

Through the Malpi Synergy Council and our 30+ corporate partners, student and faculty research is regularly framed around live questions from banks, insurers, technology firms and advisory practices, from consulting projects and field research to startup incubation.
