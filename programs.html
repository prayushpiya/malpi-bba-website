Entry requirements

Who can apply

Undergraduate (BBA, BBA Finance, BCSIT)

Completion of 10+2 or equivalent with a minimum C grade in all subjects, plus a pass in the Pokhara University entrance examination.

Postgraduate (MBA)

A Bachelor’s degree in any discipline, together with Pokhara University’s MBA entrance requirements.

Not sure which programme fits?

Call the admissions team on 01-4372986 or send us an enquiry. We will talk you through the entrance examination, the concentration options and the scholarships you may be eligible for.
