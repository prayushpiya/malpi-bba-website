The Malpi Advantage

Beyond course contents, experiential learning, foundation classes, community service and eight student-led Centres of Excellence: what a Malpi education actually looks like.

Why Malpi

What sets Malpi & Malpi-ites apart

MIC has come up with an unprecedented learning environment that helps students grow into future purpose-driven leaders. We are adding value through academic rigour and endorsement from various companies from different sectors.

The six pillars below are what a Malpi student actually experiences across four years, and they are the reason our graduates arrive in the workplace already familiar with how it works.

  • Centres of Excellence

What you actually get

Six pillars of a Malpi education

Each one runs alongside the Pokhara University syllabus rather than inside it, which is why a Malpi graduate arrives at work with more than a transcript.

01

Beyond Course Contents

Traditional approaches no longer match how students need to learn.

This initiative creates opportunities to study outside the classroom in order to build inclusive, diverse and engaging learning environments. Students work on material and problems that sit beyond the prescribed syllabus, so that a Malpi degree is measured by capability rather than coverage.

02

Experiential Learning Programmes

Applied learning built around case-based problems and simulations.

These programmes are delivered with industry experts and corporate collaborators, so students practise on the kinds of problems they will actually be handed at work. Graduates gain practical experience that gives them a real advantage when launching a career.

03

Skills and Interpersonal Development

Communication, leadership and teamwork, developed deliberately.

Alongside academic content, students build the interpersonal skills that determine how far technical knowledge actually travels: presenting, negotiating, leading a team and working across difference. This work runs through the Centre for Interpersonal & Leadership Development.

04

Foundation Classes

Closing the gap between school and a bachelor’s degree.

Foundation classes are preparatory instruction designed to assist students in closing the gap between their existing knowledge and the requirements of a bachelor’s degree. They are delivered during foundation weeks at the start of the programme, so no student begins at a disadvantage.

05

Community Service

Engagement with the communities our students come from.

Students take part in community visits during their first year as part of the community engagement and orientation programme, coordinated through the Centre for Community Collaboration. It is where the Attitude in ASK stops being an idea and becomes a habit.

06

Embracing Technology

A digitally equipped college and a job-ready graduate.

The college provides a Learning Management System, high-speed internet and online course materials, ensuring students are job-ready and technologically equipped for a workplace that assumes digital fluency as a baseline.

Student-led centres

Eight Centres of Excellence

Each centre is student-led and staff-supported, and each one exists to turn a part of the curriculum into something students actually practise.

Centre for Career Guidance and Placement

Career counselling, interview preparation and placement services. This is the centre behind our 100% placement support.

Centre for Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Supports student startups with mentorship, workspace and pitch competitions.

Centre for Information Technology

Runs coding bootcamps and hackathons for BCSIT students and anyone else who wants to build.

Centre for Banking, Finance and Insurance (BFI)

Guest lectures, trading simulations and financial workshops with our banking and insurance partners.

Centre for Marketing and Digital Excellence

Practical teaching in SEO, social media marketing and brand management.

Centre for Interpersonal and Leadership Development

Leadership camps and communication workshops across all programmes.

Centre for Sports and Hobby

Organises tournaments and fitness programmes throughout the academic year.

Centre for Community Collaboration

Facilitates volunteer programmes and social awareness initiatives.

Scholarships

Scholarships at Malpi International College

Malpi International College is offering scholarships worth NRs. 1 Crore to new undergraduate students, across academic merit, purposeful ideas and extracurricular achievement.

Soaltee Academic Excellence Award

50%–100% scholarships for four high-performing students, awarded on SEE and +2 results.

Malpi Academic Scholarship

Semester toppers receive a 100% tuition reduction; second and third place receive 50% and 25%.

IJ Foundation Purpose Scholarship

Up to 50% funding for students whose ideas demonstrate a positive impact in society.

Dean Listing Achievers Award

Recognition for students maintaining a minimum 3.7 CGPA, recognised by Pokhara University.

RB Foundation ECA/CCA Scholarship

Admissions support for students who excel in extracurricular and co-curricular activities and sports.

Eligibility and terms

Scholarships are awarded based on defined eligibility criteria and are subject to the college’s scholarship policies and guidelines. Talk to the admissions team on 01-4372986 to find out which awards you may qualify for.
