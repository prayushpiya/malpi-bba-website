MBA

A trimester-based Pokhara University postgraduate degree for working professionals and graduates moving into leadership.

Overview

Master of Business Administration

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Malpi International College, Kathmandu is a Pokhara University postgraduate programme designed for working professionals and fresh graduates who want to advance into leadership roles.

The trimester-based MBA consists of 6 terms, covering Foundation & Analytical courses, Management Core courses, and deep specialisation through one of five concentration areas: Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, IT & Business Analytics, and Entrepreneurship & Innovation.

Students choose between an Industry Track (Consulting Project + Doing Business in Nepal) or a Research Track (Graduate Thesis), allowing flexibility based on career goals. Malpi’s MBA is built on industry collaboration, case-based learning and a three-month internship in Term VI that typically leads directly to employment.

Concentration areas

Five specialisations

Each concentration comprises four courses of 2.0 credits each, taken from Term IV onward.

  • Finance
  • Marketing
  • Human Resource Management
  • IT & Business Analytics
  • Entrepreneurship & Innovation

What each specialisation covers

Finance
Institutional markets, corporate finance, investments and international finance.
Marketing
Service marketing, consumer behaviour, brand management and digital marketing.
Human Resource Management
Talent acquisition, compensation, leadership and employee relations.
IT & Business Analytics
Data mining, business intelligence and AI systems.
Entrepreneurship & Innovation
Innovation management, scaling operations and corporate development.

Industry Track or Research Track

Industry Track

A Consulting Project (3 credits) addressing a real-world business problem, plus Doing Business in Nepal (3 credits).

Research Track

A Graduate Thesis (6 credits), presented and defended in a viva-voce examination.

Both tracks conclude with the 4-credit internship in Term VI.

Answers

Frequently asked questions about MBA

What is the duration of the MBA programme at Malpi?

The MBA at Malpi International College is a trimester-based programme consisting of 6 terms (approximately 2 years). Students complete 60 credit hours including foundation courses, core management courses, concentration electives and a three-month internship.

What concentration areas are available in the MBA?

Malpi’s MBA offers five specialisations: Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Information Technology & Business Analytics, and Entrepreneurship & Innovation. Each concentration includes 4 elective courses of 2 credits each.

What is the difference between the Industry Track and the Research Track?

The Industry Track requires students to complete a Consulting Project (3 credits) plus Doing Business in Nepal (3 credits). The Research Track requires a Graduate Thesis (6 credits). Both tracks end with a 4-credit internship in Term VI.

Who should pursue an MBA at Malpi?

The MBA suits BBA, BBA Finance and BCSIT graduates looking to advance into management, working professionals seeking to move into leadership roles, and entrepreneurs wanting a stronger business foundation.

What are the entry requirements for the MBA at Malpi?

Applicants must hold a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline and meet Pokhara University’s MBA entrance requirements. Contact MIC on 01-4372986 or info@mic.edu.np for current intake details and the entrance examination schedule.

Does the MBA at Malpi include industry exposure?

Yes. The MBA includes company visits, guest lectures from industry leaders through the Malpi Business Dialogue series, real-world consulting projects, startup incubation programmes and a mandatory three-month internship in Term VI.

Can I do an evening MBA at Malpi to continue working?

Please contact Malpi on 01-4372986 to confirm current batch timings and the availability of morning, evening and weekend schedules.
