About Malpi International College

A Pokhara University affiliated college in Basundhara, Kathmandu, delivering industry-connected business, finance and technology education since 1998.

Who we are

Malpi International College at a glance

Malpi International College (MIC) is a private higher education institution located at Narayan Gopal Chowk, Basundhara, Chakrapath, Kathmandu, Nepal. Founded in 1998 by a group of academicians and industrialists, MIC is affiliated with Pokhara University and offers four programmes: BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration), BBA Finance, BCSIT (Bachelor of Computer Systems and Information Technology) and MBA (Master of Business Administration).

MIC provides 100% job placement support for graduating students, backed by partnerships with 30+ corporate companies. Annual scholarships worth over NPR 1 Crore are available to new undergraduate students. The college operates on the ASK principle, placing Attitude, Skill and Knowledge at the centre of its educational philosophy.

Direction

Our philosophy

The ASK principle

Malpi strongly believes in the ASK principle of Attitude, Skill and Knowledge, taking all three components as the core of our college values.

While we equally promote Skills and Knowledge among the students, we at Malpi keep Attitude as of paramount importance. It is the difference between a graduate who knows the answer and a graduate an employer trusts with the problem.

MIC has come up with an unprecedented learning environment that helps students grow into future purpose-driven leaders. We are adding value through academic rigour and endorsement from various companies from different sectors.

Explore the Malpi Advantage

Affiliation

An affiliated college of Pokhara University

Malpi International College is an affiliated college of Pokhara University (PU) for all of its programmes: BBA, BBA Finance, BCSIT and MBA. Every degree awarded at MIC is a Pokhara University degree, taught to the PU curriculum and assessed under PU examination rules.

Admission to all undergraduate programmes requires completion of 10+2 (or equivalent) and a pass in the Pokhara University entrance examination. Postgraduate admission to the MBA requires a bachelor’s degree in any discipline together with the University’s MBA entrance requirements.

Recognised achievement

Students maintaining a minimum 3.7 CGPA are recognised on the Dean’s List, an award recognised by Pokhara University.

Leadership

Governed by academics and industry leaders

Malpi International College was established in 1998 by a group of academicians and industrialists, and that combination still governs it today.

Our Board brings together leadership from across Nepal’s hospitality, banking, insurance, consumer goods, real estate and healthcare sectors, while the management team runs the day-to-day academic, examination and student-support functions of the college.

Partnership & Collaboration

30+ corporate partners in Nepal and internationally

Our corporate network spans banking and financial institutions, insurance, IT companies, CA and advisory firms, energy, conglomerates, automobiles and hospitality, giving students internships, live projects, guest lectures and graduate placements.

Partner organisations host internships, run guest lectures through the Malpi Business Dialogue series, set live consulting briefs, join our Synergy Council and recruit directly from our graduating cohorts.
