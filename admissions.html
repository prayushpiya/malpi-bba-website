Admissions

Entry requirements, the application process, documents and NPR 1 Crore+ in annual scholarships for BBA, BBA Finance, BCSIT and MBA.

Entry requirements

Who can apply

Undergraduate: BBA, BBA (Finance), BCSIT

  • Completion of 10+2 or an equivalent qualification
  • A minimum C grade in all subjects
  • A pass in the Pokhara University entrance examination
  • BCSIT accepts applicants from any stream

Postgraduate: MBA

  • A Bachelor’s degree in any discipline
  • Pokhara University’s MBA entrance requirements
  • Suited to working professionals as well as fresh graduates

How to apply

Five steps from enquiry to enrolment

1

Enquire

Call the college on 01-4372986, email info@mic.edu.np, or send the enquiry form on this page. Tell us which programme you are considering and we will confirm the current intake dates.

2

Check your eligibility

Undergraduate applicants need 10+2 (or equivalent) with a minimum C grade in all subjects. MBA applicants need a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline.

3

Collect your documents

Academic transcripts and character certificate, citizenship or passport, passport-size photographs, and the completed application form.

4

Interview and selection

Shortlisted candidates attend an interview with the admissions team, where we also discuss concentration choices and scholarship eligibility.

5

Enrol

Confirm your place, complete the fee formalities and join the foundation classes that begin your programme.

Checklist

Documents to bring

  • Completed Malpi International College application form
  • SEE and 10+2 (or equivalent) mark sheets and transcripts
  • Character certificate from your previous institution
  • Migration certificate, where applicable
  • Citizenship certificate or passport (copy)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Bachelor’s degree transcript and certificate (MBA applicants only)

Fees

The fee structure for each programme is not published online. Contact the admissions office on 01-4372986 or email info@mic.edu.np for the current fee structure and payment schedule.

Scholarships

NPR 1 Crore+ awarded to new students every year

Malpi International College offers scholarships worth NRs. 1 Crore to new undergraduate students across five distinct awards.

Soaltee Academic Excellence Award

50%–100% scholarships for four high-performing students, awarded on SEE and +2 results.

Malpi Academic Scholarship

Semester toppers receive a 100% tuition reduction; second and third place receive 50% and 25%.

IJ Foundation Purpose Scholarship

Up to 50% funding for students whose ideas demonstrate a positive impact in society.

Dean Listing Achievers Award

Recognition for students maintaining a minimum 3.7 CGPA, recognised by Pokhara University.

RB Foundation ECA/CCA Scholarship

Admissions support for students who excel in extracurricular and co-curricular activities and sports.

Answers

Admissions FAQ

Fees, entrance examinations, scholarships and transfers.

When do admissions open at Malpi International College?

Intake dates follow the Pokhara University academic calendar and the entrance examination schedule. Call the college on 01-4372986 for the current intake and entrance examination dates.

What are the fees for each programme?

The fee structure is not published on this website. Please contact the college directly on 01-4372986 or email info@mic.edu.np for the current fee structure, payment schedule and any applicable discounts.

Do I have to sit the Pokhara University entrance examination?

Yes. A pass in the relevant Pokhara University entrance examination is required for all four programmes: BBA, BBA Finance, BCSIT and MBA.

What grades do I need for the undergraduate programmes?

Applicants must have completed 10+2 or an equivalent qualification with a minimum C grade in all subjects. BCSIT accepts applicants from any stream.

How do scholarships work?

MIC offers scholarships worth over NPR 1 Crore to new undergraduate students each year across five awards. Scholarships are awarded on defined eligibility criteria and are subject to the college’s scholarship policies and guidelines. Discuss your eligibility with the admissions team when you apply.

Can I transfer from another college?

Transfer and credit-recognition cases are handled individually and are subject to Pokhara University regulations. Contact the admissions team on 01-4372986 to discuss your specific situation.

Does Malpi accept international students?

Please contact the college on 01-4372986 or email info@mic.edu.np to discuss international applications, equivalence of qualifications and the documentation required.

Questions before you apply?

Our admissions officers will talk you through eligibility, entrance dates and which scholarships you may qualify for.
