We are Malpi

An education built on Attitude, Skill and Knowledge

Malpi strongly believes in the ASK principle of Attitude, Skill and Knowledge, taking all three as the core of our college values.

While we equally promote Skills and Knowledge among our students, at Malpi we keep Attitude as of paramount importance. That belief shapes everything from how we teach to how we partner with industry: we have built an unprecedented learning environment that helps students grow into purpose-driven leaders, adding value through academic rigour and endorsement from companies across many sectors.

Scholarships

NPR 1 Crore+ awarded every year

Malpi International College offers scholarships worth NPR 1 Crore to new undergraduate students, across academic merit, purpose-driven ideas and extracurricular achievement.

  • Soaltee Academic Excellence Award

    50%–100% scholarships for four high-performing students, awarded on SEE and +2 results.

  • Malpi Academic Scholarship

    Semester toppers receive a 100% tuition reduction; second and third place receive 50% and 25%.

  • IJ Foundation Purpose Scholarship

    Up to 50% funding for students whose ideas demonstrate a positive impact in society.

  • Dean Listing Achievers Award

    Recognition for students maintaining a minimum 3.7 CGPA, recognised by Pokhara University.

  • RB Foundation ECA/CCA Scholarship

    Admissions support for students who excel in extracurricular and co-curricular activities and sports.

How scholarships are awarded

Scholarships are awarded on defined eligibility criteria and are subject to the college’s scholarship policies and guidelines. Speak to the admissions team on 01-4372986 to check which awards you may qualify for.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions prospective students and parents ask us most often.

What programmes does Malpi International College offer?

Malpi International College offers four programmes: BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration), BBA Finance, BCSIT (Bachelor of Computer Systems and Information Technology), and MBA (Master of Business Administration). All programmes are affiliated with Pokhara University.

Where is Malpi International College located?

MIC is located at Narayan Gopal Chowk, Basundhara, Chakrapath, Kathmandu, Nepal. The college is accessible from the Chakrapath and Basundhara areas of Kathmandu.

Is Malpi International College affiliated with Pokhara University?

Yes. Malpi International College is an affiliated college of Pokhara University (PU) for all its programmes: BBA, BBA Finance, BCSIT and MBA.

Does Malpi offer scholarships for new students?

Yes. MIC offers scholarships worth over NPR 1 Crore to new undergraduate students, including the Soaltee Academic Excellence Award, Malpi Academic Scholarship, IJ Foundation Purpose Scholarship, Dean Listing Achievers Award, and the RB Foundation ECA/CCA Scholarship. Scholarships are awarded on defined eligibility criteria and are subject to the college’s scholarship policies and guidelines.

Does Malpi International College support job placement?

Yes. MIC offers 100% placement support for graduates of the BBA, BBA Finance and BCSIT programmes, facilitated through partnerships with 30+ corporate companies.

When was Malpi International College established?

Malpi International College was established in 1998 by a group of academicians and industrialists. It has over 25 years of experience delivering quality higher education in Kathmandu, Nepal.

How can I apply to Malpi International College?

You can apply by contacting MIC directly on 01-4372986, emailing the admissions team, or filling out the enquiry form on this website. Applicants must have completed 10+2 and pass the Pokhara University entrance examination.

Ready to join Malpi?

Admissions are open for BBA, BBA Finance, BCSIT and MBA. Talk to our admissions team about entrance dates, scholarships and the application process.
