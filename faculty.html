Our Team

The board members and management team behind Malpi International College. Academics and industry leaders working together since 1998.

From the Chairman

Chairman’s Message

At Malpi, we are committed to creating an enriching learning environment where academic rigor meets practical experience. Our programs, including BCSIT, BBA, BBA-Finance, and MBA, are meticulously designed to cover all crucial aspects of their respective fields. We believe in learning beyond the classroom, incorporating hands-on experiences through internships, field visits, startup competitions, and real-world business simulations. These experiences ensure our students are not only knowledgeable but also capable of applying their skills in practical scenarios.

Recognizing the transformative impact of the AI era, our programs are tailored to equip students with cutting-edge skills and knowledge in this rapidly evolving field ensuring that graduates are prepared to excel in the modern business landscape.

Our strong industry partnerships at Malpi provide exceptional opportunities for professional growth. Collaborating closely with leading corporations and supported by our dedicated Center for Career Guidance and Placement, pave the way for our graduates to smoothly transition from academic excellence to thriving careers in reputable organizations.

At Malpi, we are committed to shaping future leaders who are innovative, ethical, and equipped to make significant contributions to society. I invite you to join us on this transformative journey and experience the exceptional education that Malpi International College offers.

Warm regards,
Ravi Bhakta Shrestha
Chairman, Malpi International College

From the Principal

Principal’s Message

At Malpi, we are committed to fostering a transformative educational experience that prepares our students for success in a rapidly evolving world. As Principal, it is my privilege to share with you our vision and commitment to excellence in education.

At Malpi International College, our approach to education is holistic and forward-thinking. We believe in providing a rigorous academic foundation combined with practical, hands-on learning experiences. Our teaching-learning approaches are designed to not only impart knowledge but also to develop critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a global perspective among our students.

Beyond the traditional curriculum, we place a strong emphasis on beyond-course activities that enrich the educational journey of our students. These activities include seminars, workshops, guest lectures, and cultural events that broaden their horizons and foster a spirit of lifelong learning.

Experiential learning is at the core of our educational philosophy. Through internships, industry collaborations, and entrepreneurial projects, our students gain real-world experience that complements their academic learning. This approach not only enhances their employability but also cultivates leadership qualities and prepares them to make meaningful contributions to society.

Our state-of-the-art college provides a conducive environment for learning and personal growth. With modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, and comprehensive library resources, we ensure that our students have access to the latest tools and technologies to support their academic pursuits.

We are proud of our diverse and dedicated faculty who are experts in their fields and are committed to nurturing the intellectual and personal development of our students. They inspire and challenge our students to reach their full potential, both inside and outside the classroom.

At Malpi International College, we instill values of integrity, responsibility, and empathy in our students, preparing them not only to excel in their careers but also to become ethical leaders and global citizens. We encourage our students to engage in community service initiatives and to embrace sustainability practices that contribute positively to the world around us.

I invite you to explore our website further to learn more about our programs, facilities, and the vibrant community that makes Malpi International College a hub of learning and innovation. Join us in this exciting journey of education and discovery.

Warm regards,
Dipak Adhikari
Principal, Malpi International College

Governance

Board Members

Malpi International College was founded in 1998 by a group of academicians and industrialists. Our Board continues that tradition, bringing leadership from across Nepal’s major sectors.

Ravi Bhakta Shrestha

Chairman

Mr. Ravi Bhakta Shrestha is a prominent figure in Nepal’s business community, currently leading IJ Group, a diverse conglomerate with interests in hospitality, banking, insurance, consumer goods, real estate, and healthcare.

Read full profile

Under his leadership, IJ Group has expanded its portfolio to include investments in Soaltee Hotel Ltd, Soaltee West End, Summit Hotel, and the Vivanta Hotel. He also serves as the Promoter Director of Unilever Nepal Ltd. and is a former President of the Federation of Nepalese Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FNCCI). Graduate in Business Administration, Mr. Shrestha has been honored with several prestigious awards, including the Prasidhha Prabal Gorkha Dakchhin Bahu ’Second’ and Knight Officer of the Order of Merit of the Republic of Italy. His strategic vision and business acumen continue to drive the group’s growth, maintaining the legacy of his father, Late Indra Bhakta Shrestha, a pioneer in Nepal’s trade and manufacturing sectors. Additionally, he is involved in charitable activities through the I.J. Charitable Trust, focusing on health and education sectors.

Sudarshan Chapagain

Director

Mr. Sudarshan Chapagain is a seasoned professional with a diverse background in finance, hospitality, and business management.

Read full profile

He is a Chartered Accountant from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nepal (ICAN) and holds a Diploma in International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) from ACCA London. He also earned a Master’s in Business Studies (MBS) and a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) from Tribhuvan University. Currently, Mr. Chapagain serves as Vice President of Soaltee Hotel Limited, which operates The Soaltee Kathmandu, a premier five-star luxury hotel. He is also a Director at Soaltee Hospitality Limited and Stream Peak International Pvt. Ltd. In addition, he is involved in ventures like Himalaya Mahalaxmi Tea Garden Pvt. Ltd., a reputed tea estate company. Passionate about education and hospitality, he serves as Chairman of Global Academy of Tourism & Hospitality Education (GATE College) and Gate Polytechnic Academy Pvt. Ltd. He is also a Director of Malpi International College Pvt. Ltd. His involvement extends to real estate, construction, and travel. As an Executive Member of the Hotel Association of Nepal (HAN) and a Member of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), he plays a key role in Nepal’s business landscape.

Sabin Joshi

Director

Mr. Joshi is a graduate of Indian School of Business, Hyderabad and has more than 18 years of experience in Banking, Portfolio Management, Private Equity and M&A managing many sectors such as Education, Hospitality, Banking etc.

Read full profile

Mr. Joshi is a recipient of Mahendra Vidhya Bhushan, an award then given for excellence performance in academics. Having started his career with Standard Chartered Bank in 2004, he currently is the Managing Director at RB Advisory and Ednopsis Holdings. He has worked as Assistant Vice President at Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited and Senior General Manager at the CG CORP Global handling Asset Management, Investment and Merger & Acquisition. He also worked as CEO for the education and Asset Management Head for the hospitality vertical of the CG group which had over 17 School/Colleges and over 80+ hotels all around the world. He has been director in many companies such as Nabil Investment Bank, India Safari Limited, Taj Safari Limited, Taj Maldives Pvt Limited, Taj Srilanka Pvt Ltd, Lumbini Bank limited, Malvern Education PLC London etc. Mr. Joshi is currently the Managing Director at RB Advisory Pvt Ltd.
