A four-year, 127-credit Pokhara University degree combining computer science, IT management and business fundamentals.

Overview

Bachelor of Computer Systems and Information Technology

The Bachelor of Computer Systems and Information Technology (BCSIT) programme at Malpi International College, Kathmandu is a four-year undergraduate degree affiliated with Pokhara University.

This comprehensive programme prepares students for careers in software development, IT management, cybersecurity, cloud computing, data science and artificial intelligence. Students complete 127 credit hours across 8 semesters, including hands-on project work and a dedicated internship.

With 100% placement support and partnerships with 10+ national and international IT companies, including Code Himalaya, Smart Solutions, Xuno and ComplianceQuest. BCSIT graduates from MIC are career-ready from day one.

Concentration areas

Choose one concentration from Year 3

BCSIT at Malpi offers five concentration tracks. You select your concentration from Year 3, taking four concentration courses across Semesters V to VII.

  • Computing
  • Data Science
  • Networking & Cybersecurity
  • Management Science & Systems
  • Multimedia Technology

Technology covered in the core curriculum

The BCSIT core already covers the technologies employers are hiring for: Artificial Intelligence (CMP 471) in Semester V, Cloud Computing (CMP 382) in Semester VI, and Information System Security (CMP 472) in Semester VII, alongside databases, networks, operating systems, computer graphics and software engineering.

  • Programming in C and Java, data structures and algorithms
  • Database management, object-oriented analysis and design
  • Data communication, networks and information system security
  • Artificial intelligence, cloud computing and the digital economy
  • Business grounding in management, finance, economics and digital marketing

Answers

Frequently asked questions about BCSIT

What is BCSIT and what does it stand for?

BCSIT stands for Bachelor of Computer Systems and Information Technology. It is a four-year undergraduate programme (8 semesters, 127 credit hours) offered by Pokhara University that combines computer science, IT management and business fundamentals.

How long is the BCSIT programme at Malpi?

The BCSIT programme is four years, divided into 8 semesters. Students complete coursework, project work and a final internship before graduating.

Does Malpi’s BCSIT programme support job placement?

Yes. Malpi International College offers 100% placement support for BCSIT graduates. Placements are facilitated through the college’s Centre for Career Guidance and Placement and its 30+ corporate partners.

What concentration areas can I choose in BCSIT?

BCSIT at Malpi offers five concentration tracks: Computing, Data Science, Networking & Cybersecurity, Management Science & Systems, and Multimedia Technology. You select your concentration from Year 3.

Is BCSIT better than BCA in Nepal?

BCSIT (Pokhara University, 4 years) covers a broader curriculum combining IT, business management and industry exposure with a mandatory internship. BCA (Tribhuvan University) is more computing-focused. BCSIT students benefit from structured corporate partnerships and placement support, making it a strong option for career-focused students.

What are the admission requirements for BCSIT?

To apply for BCSIT at Malpi you must have completed 10+2 (or equivalent) in any stream with a minimum C grade in all subjects. You must also pass the Pokhara University entrance examination. Contact MIC on 01-4372986 for current intake dates.

What IT companies does Malpi partner with for BCSIT placements?

MIC partners with 10+ national and international IT companies including Smart Solutions, Code Himalaya, ComplianceQuest, Xuno, Cypher Technology and AMNIL Technologies, ensuring BCSIT graduates have access to real company placements.

Does the BCSIT curriculum include AI and cloud computing?

Yes. BCSIT at Malpi includes Artificial Intelligence (CMP 471) in Semester V and Cloud Computing (CMP 382) in Semester VI. The Data Science concentration also includes AI and machine learning.
