BBA (Finance)

A specialised four-year Pokhara University degree in finance, banking, insurance and capital markets.

Overview

BBA in Finance

The BBA (Finance) programme at Malpi International College is curated to provide a sound knowledge base, conceptual understanding and skills in the area of business and management with a specific focus on finance, banking and insurance.

Across four years and eight semesters, students build the general business foundation of a BBA and then specialise deeply, choosing three courses from a major stream and two from a minor stream across Corporate Finance, Banking, Insurance, and Investment & Capital Markets.

With a 6–8 week internship, dual supervision from college and company, and a corporate network spanning Nepal’s banks and insurers, graduates enter the financial sector already knowing how it runs.

What each stream covers

Corporate Finance
Emerging Concepts in Corporate Management, SME Financial Management, International Finance, Behavioural Finance, Municipal Finance.
Banking
Emerging Banking Concepts, Treasury Management, Investment Banking, Commercial Bank Management, Central Banking & Monetary Policy, Sustainable Banking, Microfinance, Credit Risk Management.
Insurance
Risk Management, Life & Health Insurance, Property & Liability Insurance, Emerging Insurance Concepts, Microinsurance, Social Insurance, Reinsurance, Insurance Company Management, Actuarial Science.
Investment & Capital Markets
Market Concepts, Securities & Portfolio Analysis, Mutual and Pension Funds, Private Equity, Asset Management, Merchant Banking.

Electives available

Critical Thinking · Philanthropy · Environmental Studies · Media & PR · Sustainable Development · Family Business Management · Econometrics · Event Management · Technology Management · Project Management.

Answers

Frequently asked questions about BBA (Finance)

How is BBA (Finance) different from the general BBA?

Both are four-year Pokhara University degrees, but BBA (Finance) is curated to provide a sound knowledge base, conceptual understanding and skills in business and management with a specific focus on finance, banking and insurance. Its concentration streams are Corporate Finance, Banking, Insurance, and Investment & Capital Markets, and students take five specialisation courses (15 credit hours) rather than the general BBA’s three.

What is the duration of BBA (Finance) at Malpi?

Four years across 8 semesters of coursework, project work and internship. Students enrol in five courses every semester, and each course carries a minimum of about 48 contact hours.

When does the internship take place?

Students undertake a 6–8 week internship during Semester VI, and the internship carries 6 credits in Semester VIII. Internships are facilitated through the Centre for Career Guidance and Placement with dual supervision from the college and the host company.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Students must have completed 10+2 (or equivalent) with a minimum C grade in all subjects and must pass the Pokhara University entrance examination. Contact MIC on 01-4372986 for intake dates.

What careers does BBA (Finance) lead to?

Graduates pursue positions as bankers, investment analysts, insurance officers, stock market analysts, financial advisors, auditors and risk managers across financial institutions, banks, insurance companies and capital markets.

Does Malpi have partnerships in the financial sector?

Yes. Our corporate network includes Siddhartha Bank, Muktinath Sewa Bank, Kamana Sewa Bikas Bank, Nepal Life Insurance, Sanima Reliance Life Insurance, United Ajod Life Insurance, Lumbini Insurance, SMTM Capital and IME Pay, among others.

What happens outside the classroom?

The Centre for Banking, Finance and Insurance runs finance clubs, workshops, case studies, trading simulations and networking events alongside the taught curriculum.
