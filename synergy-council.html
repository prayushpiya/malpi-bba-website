Malpi Synergy Council

Four specialist councils pairing faculty with senior practitioners, keeping the curriculum aligned with how Nepal’s industries actually work.

Academia meets industry

Bridging the gap between the classroom and the company

The Malpi Synergy Council serves a crucial role in bridging the gap between academia and industry at Malpi International College.

It brings together distinguished members who combine industry and academic expertise, and who convene regularly to discuss emerging trends and align our curriculum with professional practice. The council sits across four specialist areas, each pairing faculty with senior practitioners from the sector concerned.

Council for BFIs

Enriches the finance curriculum through collaboration between financial institutions and academic leaders, so what is taught keeps pace with how banks and financial institutions actually operate.

Why this matters to a student

A curriculum reviewed by the people who hire from it ages more slowly. The council is the reason subjects such as fintech, blockchain, digital payments, HR analytics and business analytics appear in Malpi’s teaching rather than waiting for the next syllabus revision.
