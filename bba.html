Overview

Bachelor of Business Administration

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) programme at Malpi International College, Kathmandu is a four-year, 8-semester degree affiliated with Pokhara University.

Designed to produce entrepreneurial managers and business leaders, the BBA combines theoretical foundations with hands-on practical training through projects, internships and beyond-course activities. Students choose from five concentration areas (Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management, Accounting, and IT & Management Science), allowing deep specialisation alongside core business skills.

With 100% placement support and corporate connections spanning banking, finance, marketing and entrepreneurship, Malpi BBA graduates are prepared to lead in Nepal’s growing economy.

Specialise

Concentration areas

Choose one concentration from Year 3

Malpi’s BBA offers five concentration areas. You specialise from Year 3 onward, taking three concentration courses alongside two electives.

  • Accounting
  • Marketing
  • Finance
  • Human Resource Management
  • IT & Management Science (ICT)

What each concentration covers

Accounting
Auditing, Taxation, Advanced Management Accounting, Management Control Systems, Accounting Reports, Accounting for Project Management.
Marketing
Digital and Social Media Marketing, Promotion Management, Sales Management, Retail Marketing, Service Marketing, Brand Management, Rural Marketing, Marketing Research.
Finance
Corporate Finance, Cooperatives Management, Microfinance, Investment Management, International Finance, Risk Management, Financial Derivatives, Financial Institutions, Sustainable Finance.
Human Resource Management
Labour Laws, Contemporary HRM Issues, Compensation & Benefits, Negotiation & Conflict Management, Performance Appraisal, HR Analytics, HR Development.
Entrepreneurship
Start-up Finance, Small Business Management, Entrepreneurial Finance, Innovation Management, Social Entrepreneurship, Venture Models, Rural Entrepreneurship, Online Business, Agri-Business, New Product Development.
IT & Management Science
Digital Economy, Business Analytics, Enterprise Resource Planning, IT Governance, Cyber Security, Supply Chain Analytics, Business Process Management, Technology Management, Project Management, Event Management.

Electives (select two)

Critical Thinking · Environmental Ethics · Philanthropy · Social Ecology · Environmental Studies · Sustainable Development · Family Business Management · Society & Politics · Basic Econometrics · Media & PR · Energy & Sustainability · Technology Management · Population Dynamics.

Answers

Frequently asked questions about BBA

What is the BBA programme duration at Malpi?

The BBA at Malpi is a four-year programme (8 semesters). Students complete coursework, practicum, project work and an internship in Semester VIII before graduating.

What concentration areas can I choose in BBA?

Malpi’s BBA programme offers concentrations in Accounting, Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management, and IT & Management Science (ICT). You can specialise from Year 3 onward.

Does the BBA programme at Malpi support job placement?

Yes. All BBA graduates receive 100% placement support through the Centre for Career Guidance and Placement, with access to 30+ corporate partner companies.

What are the entry requirements for BBA at Malpi?

Students must have completed 10+2 (or equivalent) with a minimum C grade in all subjects and must pass the Pokhara University entrance examination. Contact MIC on 01-4372986 for intake dates.

Is BBA better than BBS in Nepal?

BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) is more industry-oriented and globally recognised compared to BBS (Bachelor of Business Studies, TU). BBA focuses on practical business skills, international case studies and industry internships, making it well suited to students targeting careers in private companies, banking and entrepreneurship.

Does Malpi’s BBA include digital marketing?

Yes. The BBA curriculum includes a Digital Marketing Practicum (PRC 112) in Semester III, plus a Marketing concentration with courses such as Digital and Social Media Marketing, Sales Management and Brand Management.

Can I pursue an MBA after BBA at Malpi?

Yes. BBA graduates are eligible to apply for the MBA programme at Malpi International College (Pokhara University) or at other accredited universities in Nepal and abroad.
